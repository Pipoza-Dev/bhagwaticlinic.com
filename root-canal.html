Save your natural teeth with computerized micro-endodontics. Led by Dr. Tanu Gupta, MDS in Conservative Dentistry & Endodontics with 18+ years of clinical mastery.

Micro-Endodontic Specialty

Save Your Natural Tooth Painlessly

When dental decay reaches the innermost chamber of a tooth (the dental pulp), bacteria cause inflammation, severe throbbing pain, and dental abscesses. In the past, such teeth were routinely extracted.

As an Endodontist (MDS), Dr. Tanu Gupta specializes in thoroughly cleansing infected root canal systems, disinfecting micro-branches, and sealing them with bio-compatible gutta-percha. With rotary nickel-titanium instrumentation and computerized apex locators, the procedure is completely comfortable and often completed in just a single sitting.

Why Save Your Natural Tooth?

No artificial replacement, regardless of how advanced, beats your natural biological root in sensory proprioception and natural chewing sensation.

When Do You Need an RCT?

If you experience any of the following symptoms, schedule a clinical checkup promptly before the infection spreads:

  • Severe Throbbing Pain: Pain that spikes while chewing, biting down, or lying flat at night.
  • Lingering Temperature Sensitivity: Sharp pain from hot tea/coffee or cold water lasting long after the drink is finished.
  • Pimple / Swelling on Gum: A localized bubble or swelling near the tooth root draining pus or tasting salty.
  • Tooth Discoloration: Dark gray or black discoloration of a tooth following physical trauma or deep cavity.
Clinical Protocol

The Single-Sitting RCT Procedure

How Dr. Tanu Gupta performs painless, micro-precision root canal therapy in Haridwar.

01

Profound Anesthesia

Gentle topical gel followed by modern computerized local anesthesia. The entire tooth and surrounding tissue are rendered completely numb.

02

Pulp Cleansing & Apex Locating

Infected pulp tissue is gently removed. Electronic apex locators measure canal length with 99.8% accuracy without painful guesswork.

03

Rotary Shaping & Disinfection

Ultra-flexible rotary nickel-titanium (NiTi) files clean and shape curved root canals while sterile irrigants eliminate bacteria.

04

3D Hermetic Sealing

Canals are densely filled with biocompatible gutta-percha, followed by post-and-core strengthening and a custom Zirconia protective crown.
