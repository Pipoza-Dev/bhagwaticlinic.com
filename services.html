Single-Sitting Root Canal Treatment (RCT)

  • Painless computerized apex locators
  • Flexible rotary NiTi instrumentation
  • Preserves natural tooth roots & chewing bite

Dental Implantology & Full Rehabilitation

Permanent bio-compatible titanium implants that anchor directly to the jawbone. Replace single, multiple, or full dental arches with lifelong stability and zero damage to adjacent teeth.

  • Single tooth & multi-tooth implants
  • Full-mouth implant rehabilitation
  • Fixed CAD/CAM Zirconia prosthetic crowns

Crowns, Fixed Bridges & Custom Dentures

Protect brittle, treated teeth and fill gaps in your smile. We provide metal-free Zirconia crowns, porcelain-fused-to-metal (PFM) caps, fixed bridges, and flexible partial dentures.

  • High-strength monolithic Zirconia
  • Natural shade & translucency matching
  • Flexible, comfortable unbreakable dentures

Ultrasonic Scaling & Gum Therapy

Painless, ultrasonic plaque, tartar (calculus), and stubborn nicotine/tea stain removal. Cleans deep into gum pockets to prevent bleeding gums and eliminate bad breath.

  • Gentle ultrasonic tips (enamel safe)
  • Stain polishing & breath freshening
  • Reverses gingivitis & gum swelling
Book Consultation (₹200)

Tooth-Coloured Composite Fillings

100% mercury-free aesthetic composite resin that blends with your natural tooth enamel. Instantly hardened under blue LED light to seal dental cavities and repair minor chips.

  • Invisible natural tooth shade blending
  • Post-and-core structural reinforcement
  • Immediate normal eating & drinking
Book Consultation (₹200)

Painless Extractions & Wisdom Tooth Surgery

Atraumatic tooth removal for non-restorable roots and painful impacted third molars (wisdom teeth). Administered under gentle local anesthesia with smooth post-op recovery.

  • Profound, painless local anesthesia
  • Minimally invasive surgical protocol
  • Comprehensive aftercare & medication guide
