Permanent Dental Implants & Prosthetics

Restore missing teeth with bio-compatible titanium implants that look, chew, and feel just like natural teeth. Certified surgical care in Jwalapur, Haridwar.

Permanent Tooth Replacement

The Gold Standard in Modern Dentistry

A dental implant is a precision medical-grade titanium screw surgically placed into your jawbone. Over a brief healing period, it fuses seamlessly with the natural bone tissue (a biological marvel known as osseointegration).

Unlike traditional dental bridges which require trimming healthy adjacent teeth, or loose removable dentures that slip and irritate gums, dental implants stand independently. They preserve jawbone volume, prevent facial sunkenness, and allow you to chew apples, nuts, and rotis with total confidence.

98% Clinical Success

Backed by decades of worldwide dental research and clinical longevity.

Lifetime Durability

With regular oral hygiene and checkups, implants can last your lifetime.

Treatment Roadmap

Your Dental Implant Journey

Step-by-step guidance from initial ₹200 consultation to final Zirconia crown delivery.

01

Diagnosis & 3D Imaging

Initial ₹200 consultation with Dr. Tanu Gupta. Digital X-rays evaluate your bone height, nerve canal distance, and medical fitness.

02

Painless Surgical Placement

Under gentle local anesthesia, the medical titanium implant fixture is placed into the jawbone with micrometer surgical precision.

03

Bone Osseointegration

During the healing period, your natural jawbone cells integrate with the titanium surface, forming a rock-solid biological anchor.

04

Custom Crown Attachment

A custom CAD/CAM monolithic Zirconia or ceramic crown is securely placed, perfectly matching your bite and natural smile contour.

Answers

Frequently Asked Questions on Implants

Common queries about pain, eligibility, and aftercare.

Surprisingly, most patients report that receiving a dental implant involves less discomfort than a standard tooth extraction! The procedure is conducted under profound local anesthesia. Any minor post-procedure soreness is easily managed with mild pain medication for 1–2 days.

Almost any adult in general good health with sufficient jawbone density can safely receive dental implants. Controlled diabetics, hypertensive patients, and seniors routinely undergo successful implant procedures. Dr. Tanu Gupta will assess your individual suitability during your initial ₹200 consultation.

Just like your natural teeth! Regular twice-daily brushing, daily flossing, and a routine professional cleaning at our clinic every 6 months keep your gums healthy and your implants secure for a lifetime.
