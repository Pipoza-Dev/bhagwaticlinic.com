Academic & Clinical Background

Clinical Mastery & Compassionate Dentistry

Dr. Tanu Gupta earned her Bachelor of Dental Surgery (BDS) in 2008 from Dr. B.R. Ambedkar University, followed by an intensive Master of Dental Surgery (MDS) in Conservative Dentistry & Endodontics in 2013 from the prestigious Bapuji College of Dental Sciences, Davangere — one of India's top dental institutes.

Having completed extensive post-graduate advanced certifications in Dental Implantology, she combines the art of micro-endodontic root canal treatments (saving natural teeth) with permanent titanium implant restorations (replacing lost teeth) to provide complete rehabilitation for Haridwar residents.

Endodontics Expertise

Mastery in complex rotary canal instrumentation, curved canals, retreatment, and single-sitting RCTs.

Implantology Certification

Surgical placement of titanium implants, bone preservation, and CAD/CAM custom prosthetics.

Infrastructure & Hygiene

Clean Operatory & Infection Control

We maintain rigorous hospital-level sanitization protocols and comfortable onsite patient amenities.

01

Class-B Vacuum Autoclave

Every reusable instrument undergoes ultrasonic washing and high-temperature vacuum steam autoclaving in sealed sterile pouches, opened right in front of the patient.

02

Ergonomic Operatory Dental Chair

Our fully equipped modern operatory features an ergonomic patient chair with integrated fiber-optic handpieces and micro-suction for anxiety-free procedures.

03

Dedicated Patient Restroom Access

A dedicated, hygienic, and sanitized onsite restroom is available directly within the facility for the comfort and dignity of patients and accompanying family members.

04

Digital Apex Locators & Rotary NiTi

High-precision electronic root canal length measuring systems eliminate guesswork and multiple sittings, making root canals precise and painless.

05

100% Disposable Exam Kits

Gloves, masks, patient drapes, suction tips, and irrigation needles are strictly single-use and disposed of in medical-grade bio-waste bins.

06

Air-Conditioned Waiting Lounge

A soothing, air-conditioned waiting environment with drinking water and zero long waiting times due to scheduled slot allocations.

Our Core Ethics

Patient-First Philosophy

At Bhagwati Dental Clinic, our medical approach is built on three unwavering principles:

  • Preserving Natural Teeth: Extraction is always the last resort. We exhaust every advanced endodontic treatment to save your natural biological teeth.
  • Transparent Fee Structure: Complete honesty before any treatment begins. Initial consultation is strictly ₹200, with no hidden laboratory charges.
  • Painless Clinical Experience: Gentle local anesthesia techniques and modern rotary instruments ensure you never dread visiting the dentist again.
