Meet Your Doctor

Dr. Tanu Gupta

Endodontist, Aesthetic Dental Specialist & Certified Implant Surgeon

With over 18 years of clinical dental experience, Dr. Tanu Gupta completed her BDS in 2008 and her Master of Dental Surgery (MDS) in Conservative Dentistry & Endodontics from the prestigious Bapuji College of Dental Sciences in 2013.

Her practice philosophy focuses on saving natural teeth whenever possible. Using rotary nickel-titanium instrumentation and computerized apex locators, she delivers virtually painless root canal procedures in single visits, while restoring missing teeth with lifelong dental implants.
